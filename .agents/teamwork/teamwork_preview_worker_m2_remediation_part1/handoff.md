### 1.2 ZATCA TLV QR Tag 9 Ambiguity & 1-Byte Length Overflow (Doc 03)
- In Doc 03 §4.5 line 698, Tag 9 was labeled:
  `| 9 | ZATCA CA Certificate Stamp | Binary | Digital Certificate issued by ZATCA CA |`
- A standard X.509 digital certificate spans 600 to 1,500 bytes (averaging ~850 bytes).
- The binary TLV encoder writes length as a single unsigned octet:
  `buffer[offset++] = value.length;`
- An 850-byte payload truncates modulo 256:
  $$850 \pmod{256} = 82$$
  This causes 768 bytes of certificate payload to be misread as subsequent corrupt TLV tags, breaking QR code decoders on mobile and tax authority scanners.
